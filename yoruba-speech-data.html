Technical and language requirements

Agree the language treatment and deliverables before collection starts.

Each production scope is documented around audio format, transcript treatment, tonal orthography, metadata, quality checks and delivery method. The exact package is agreed in the project statement of work or licence.

Typical audio and transcript options

  • WAV audio, 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM; 48 kHz studio option where agreed
  • Verbatim and time-aligned transcript options
  • Yorùbá transcripts with full tonal diacritics and Unicode NFC normalisation
  • JSON, TXT or CSV delivery formats to brief

Quality and documentation

  • Native-speaker review of 100% of Yorùbá transcriptions
  • Inter-annotator agreement of ≥ 80% (word-level or Cohen’s κ per project), measured on a 10% blind sample per batch and reported in the batch QC report
  • Free re-delivery on any batch failing agreed QA thresholds (up to two re-deliveries per batch)
  • Audio screening for signal quality, clipping and duplication where relevant to the agreed delivery
  • Data-card, licence and consent documentation materials for the delivery

Frequently asked questions

Questions before evaluating Yorùbá speech data.

Is Yorùbá speech data available to evaluate now?

BSG DataWorks has Yorùbá speech and time-aligned transcription support available for technical evaluation. Contact us with your intended use so we can discuss the relevant materials and the appropriate commercial path.

Can transcripts include tonal diacritics?

Yes. Yorùbá transcripts include full tonal diacritics and Unicode NFC normalisation by default. State any additional transcription, time-alignment, annotation and delivery-format requirements in your initial brief.

Can we commission a Yorùbá collection to our specification?

Yes. A commissioned discussion can cover the speech scenario, speaker profile, recording environment, target volume, transcript and metadata requirements, delivery timeline, rights and quality criteria.

How do commercial rights and consent documentation work?

Commercial use, permitted sublicensing and the documentation package are addressed in the applicable licence or project agreement. BSG DataWorks collects and maintains contributor-consent records consistent with the agreed scope.
