About BSG DataWorks

Teaching AI to understand African voices — ethically, and from the ground up.

BSG DataWorks is committed to building ethically sourced, human-centred datasets for AI systems that need to work for African users. We collect, curate, and annotate human-sourced speech, text, image, and video in African-accented English and indigenous African languages—data that has often been under-represented in the material used to train and evaluate AI.

The gap we exist to close

Data that better reflects African voices, languages, and contexts.

Modern AI learns from examples. Yet many widely available datasets give greater weight to American and European varieties of speech and language, leaving African accents, languages, and everyday contexts under-represented. As a result, systems may not reliably reflect how Africans speak and communicate. African languages require deliberate, properly documented collection. That is the gap BSG DataWorks exists to help close.

What we do

Collection, preparation, and licensing support for African AI data.

We provide African-language and African-accented datasets across four data types: speech and voice, text, image, and video. Our work is available through commissioned collection, annotation and labelling, and commercial dataset licensing. Nigerian-accented English and Yorùbá speech samples are available for evaluation; Igbo, Hausa, and Nigerian Pidgin are in the collection pipeline.

We help organisations obtain African data that reflects real languages, markets, and user contexts, supported by documented contributor consent, traceable provenance, and practical quality workflows. Based in Nigeria and working across West Africa, BSG combines linguistic-network experience built since 2005 with focused data collection, preparation, and compliance support for technical and legal teams. Our initial specialization is Nigerian English and Yorùbá, with plans to expand into Nigerian Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa, Kanuri and other African languages.

BSG DataWorks was founded by Olayemi Olabenjo, Managing Consultant. For more than two decades, he has led Nigerian language-operations work spanning translation, localisation, transcription, speech-data collection, and annotation. This gives BSG a practical foundation for organising the people, language expertise, consent records, and quality checks that an African data-collection project requires.

His experience connects African-language expertise and contributor-network coordination with the AI and data-science requirements behind training and evaluation data. The result is an operating approach that treats collection, annotation, documentation, and quality assurance as connected parts of one delivery process.

The founder is joined by four fellow directors, three of whom are software engineers. Together with a small technical team, they provide the operational and technical support behind collection, quality control, and delivery.

Together, the team is focused on doing this work properly: with genuine consent, careful curation, and compliance built in from the start.
