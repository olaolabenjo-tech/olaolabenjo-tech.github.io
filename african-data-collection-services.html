Data types and use cases

Collect the human-sourced data your AI system needs to learn from.

Collection design should reflect the target model behaviour, not simply the available format. We scope the relevant data type, scenario and contributor profile with you before collection begins.

Speech and voice

Collect scripted, conversational-style, dialogue, voice-over or domain-specific speech with the transcript, time-alignment and metadata requirements agreed for the project.

Text and language

Scope written, transcribed, bilingual, prompt-response or instruction data around the language treatment and use case required for low-resource NLP and language systems.

Image and video

Plan consented image or scenario-based video capture around the required objects, environments, activities, devices, angles and lighting variation.

Custom and combined projects

Use a coordinated brief where the work needs more than one modality, such as speech with image, video with structured tags, or collection with downstream annotation.

Quality, rights and delivery

Agree the evidence behind the data before collection starts.

Each collection scope documents the technical specification, contributor requirements, quality criteria, documentation package and delivery path. The final package is agreed in the applicable proposal, statement of work or licence.

Collection specification

  • Language, domain, scenario, contributor and device requirements to brief
  • Audio, image or video formats and capture variation agreed for the project
  • Transcript, metadata and annotation requirements where applicable
  • Quality criteria and reporting defined before delivery

Rights and documentation

  • Written, informed contributor consent covering the agreed commercial use
  • Contributor records and data provenance retained for the agreed scope
  • Data-card, licence and consent documentation materials for the delivery
  • Client-specified secure transfer or another approved delivery destination

Frequently asked questions

Questions teams ask before commissioning data collection.

Can you collect data to our custom specification?

Yes. Start with the target model use, data type, language or market, participant profile, scenario, device requirements, volume and timeline. BSG DataWorks will discuss the appropriate collection and commercial route.

Which data types can you collect?

Current collection scope includes human-sourced speech, text and language data, image data and scenario-based video capture. The final scope is agreed around the requested use case and collection specification.

How are contributor rights and consent handled?

BSG DataWorks obtains written, informed contributor consent covering the agreed commercial scope and maintains contributor and consent documentation as part of the collection and delivery process.

What should we send in the first collection brief?

Tell us the intended model use, data type, languages or markets, target participant profile, target volume, technical format, desired timeline, governance requirements and approximate budget or licensing range if known.
